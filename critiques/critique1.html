Critique 001: Legacy Network Infrastructure Evaluation

Evaluated: September 18, 2026 by Lead Auditor

1. Executive Summary

An assessment of the legacy subsystem reveals critical bottlenecks in data routing efficiency. Current terminal configurations fail to meet minimum compliance thresholds outlined in Directive 404-B.

2. Core Findings

Through systematic observation, packet loss rates exceed acceptable margins during peak operational hours. Hardware replacement is deemed mandatory.

3. Action Items

Operators must log all hardware serial numbers before scheduling maintenance windows.
